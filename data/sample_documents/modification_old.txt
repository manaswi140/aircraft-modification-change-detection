
MODIFICATION ID: MOD-SAMPLE-001
TITLE: Environmental Control System Inspection Requirements
REVISION: A

REQ-001: Inspect the environmental control system ducts every 500 flight hours.

REQ-002: Replace damaged duct insulation before the aircraft returns to service.

REQ-003: Record all inspection results in the aircraft maintenance logbook.

REQ-004: Perform inspections using the approved maintenance procedure.

REQ-005: Notify the maintenance supervisor when a defect is identified.
