
MODIFICATION ID: MOD-SAMPLE-001
TITLE: Environmental Control System Inspection Requirements
REVISION: C

REQ-101: Inspect the environmental control system ducts every 300 flight hours.

REQ-102: Replace damaged duct insulation before the aircraft returns to service.

REQ-103: Record all inspection results in the aircraft maintenance logbook and the digital maintenance tracking system.

REQ-104: Perform inspections using the approved maintenance procedure.

REQ-105: Notify the maintenance supervisor and quality assurance team when a defect is identified.

REQ-106: Verify that inspection records are complete before closing the maintenance task.
